Team overview

One head, eight functions, two operating axes

Global IT Risk is led by a single Head overseeing eight functions. Six are global — Governance, Operational Risk, Shared Services, Third Party Risk, Regulatory/Audit/Issues Management, and Compliance Assessments — each setting a standard or running a program that applies everywhere. Two are regional — US IT Risk and EMEA IT Risk — executing those programs in-market, adapting for local regulatory requirements. Together the eight functions break into 30 subteams, the level services and their processes will attach to next.

How to read this

One org, two axes

Global functions define the “what”

Governance, Operational Risk, Shared Services, Third Party Risk, Regulatory/Audit/Issues Management, and Compliance Assessments each set a standard or run a program that applies everywhere, regardless of region.

Regional functions own the “where”

US IT Risk and EMEA IT Risk execute those six programs in‑market, adapting for local regulatory requirements, and are accountable for the result there.
